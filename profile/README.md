# Tabbio

**The one CV that gets you seen.**

Tabbio is one verified CV at your own link that recruiters, apps, and any AI can read. Verified with UAE PASS in the UAE. Built for the Gulf, in Arabic and English. Founded by [Ahmed AlDhraif](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif), one of the most-followed HR voices in the UAE.

- One CV at your own link: yourname.tabbio.com
- Connect a verified CV to any AI
- A career agent that drafts, and a person who approves every send

Building, sharing, and downloading your CV is free.
